# Africa Regulatory, Compliance and Tax Dossiers (54 Countries)

**Author:** Modus Chora Studio · **Updated:** 2026-07-31 · **Status:** planning reference — verify before acting

This subfolder holds one dossier per African Union member state (54 countries) covering: regulatory authorities, central banks, compliance steps per Sita Sector industry, tax planning parameters, and official and dominant spoken languages.

## Citation convention

When the [Sita Sector Energy Playbook](../sita-sector-energy-playbook.md) (or any other wiki page) refers to a country-specific step or play, cite the dossier inline as:

```markdown
[Country dossier: Kenya](kenya.md) · [Country dossier: Nigeria](nigeria.md)
```

Each dossier repeats its citation key in its header. The playbook's country-specific sections link to the relevant dossiers instead of restating country detail.

## Schema

Every dossier follows [_template.md](_template.md): Overview (capital, currency, monetary zone, regional blocs, languages) · Central bank and financial regulation · Energy sector · Sector compliance steps (six Sita sectors) · Tax planning · Expansion notes.

## Important caveats

- Fiscal figures are standard or commonly-cited rates as at mid-2026; finance acts change them frequently. Always verify with the national revenue authority.
- Regulator names, mandates and licensing thresholds change. The energy-sector column marks where the current regulator should be confirmed with the national ministry.
- Dossiers are a planning baseline, not legal, tax or financial advice. Nothing here replaces engagement of local counsel.
