## 2. Central bank and financial regulation

**Central bank:** Banque des États de l'Afrique Centrale (BEAC). Fintech ventures should plan on central-bank licensing for payment and lending products, AML/CFT registration, and consumer-credit rules. Cross-border digital services are increasingly taxed or regulated by regional frameworks (WAEMU/ECOWAS/EAC harmonisation).

**Data protection:** CNPD (Commission Nationale pour la Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel) — confirm the current regulator and registration thresholds before launching consumer-facing services.

## 3. Energy sector

**Regulator / authority:** ARSEL (Agence de Régulation du Secteur de l'Électricité). Verify licensing thresholds for generation, the existence of net-metering or open-access rules, tariff structure (energy charge, demand charge, time-of-use), and any interconnection regime before building energy assets.

## 4. Sector compliance steps

The six Sita Sector priorities map to the following first-line regulators and steps (generic framework; verify the exact authority names and current fees):

| Sector | Primary compliance steps |
|---|---|
| **Fintech** | CNPD (Commission Nationale pour la Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel) licensing for payments and lending; AML/CFT compliance; data protection registration (19.25%); consumer protection. |
| **Healthtech** | Ministry of Health approval for products and services; medicines/pharmacy board where applicable; data protection for sensitive health data; medical device registration where relevant. |
| **Edtech** | Ministry of Education content and curriculum accreditation; data protection with specific attention to minors' data; intellectual property and content licensing. |
| **Agritech** | Ministry of Agriculture and plant-health authority (phytosanitary); land use and lease diligence; export/customs compliance where cross-border. |
| **Retail & ecommerce** | National revenue authority (VAT and digital-service taxes); consumer-protection authority; data protection at consumer scale; standards bureau for product compliance. |
| **Energy & manufacturing** | Energy regulator: 30% (33% for some sectors); environmental impact assessment (EIA); standards bureau certification; land/lease and utility agreements; special economic zone / export-processing incentives where available. |

## 5. Tax planning

| Item | Value (verify with national revenue authority) |
|---|---|
| Standard corporate income tax | 30% (33% for some sectors) |
| Standard VAT / sales tax | 19.25% |

**Investment promotion agency:** Cameroon Investment Promotion Agency (API). **Incentives:** Finance Act incentives; special regimes.

Tax-planning considerations: holding-structure choices (local subsidiary vs branch), withholding taxes on dividends/interest/royalties (commonly 5–15%, verify per treaty), transfer-pricing documentation, digital-service taxes where enacted, and export-processing/special economic zone regimes where available. All rates require verification against the current national finance act or equivalent.
