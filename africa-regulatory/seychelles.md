## 2. Central bank and financial regulation

**Central bank:** Central Bank of Seychelles. Fintech ventures should plan on central-bank licensing for payment and lending products, AML/CFT registration, and consumer-credit rules. Cross-border digital services are increasingly taxed or regulated by regional frameworks (WAEMU/ECOWAS/EAC harmonisation).

**Data protection:** Verify national authority — confirm the current regulator and registration thresholds before launching consumer-facing services.

## 3. Energy sector

**Regulator / authority:** Seychelles Energy Commission (SEC). Verify licensing thresholds for generation, the existence of net-metering or open-access rules, tariff structure (energy charge, demand charge, time-of-use), and any interconnection regime before building energy assets.

| Sector | Primary compliance steps |

## 5. Tax planning

| Item | Value (verify with national revenue authority) |
|---|---|
| Standard corporate income tax | 25% |
| Standard VAT / sales tax | 15% |

**Investment promotion agency:** Seychelles Investment Board (SIB). **Incentives:** Offshore financial services; tourism-linked incentives.

Tax-planning considerations: holding-structure choices (local subsidiary vs branch), withholding taxes on dividends/interest/royalties (commonly 5–15%, verify per treaty), transfer-pricing documentation, digital-service taxes where enacted, and export-processing/special economic zone regimes where available. All rates require verification against the current national finance act or equivalent.
