## How this wiki relates to the Sita Sector Program

The Sita Sector Program prioritizes six sectors: **fintech, healthtech, edtech, agritech, retail & ecommerce, energy & manufacturing.** The [Energy Playbook](sita-sector-energy-playbook.md) is the strategic extension of that program into energy as an *operating layer*: power is both the hidden cost (margin lever) and the hidden constraint (scale lever) in each sector.

Operational source-of-truth figures (tier pricing, Legal Health Check, compliance angles, finder's fee / DD fee, compensation model, Compass schema) live in the skill references at `.opencode/skills/mc-studio-legal-ops/references/` — the wiki links to them where relevant rather than duplicating them.

Cross-border work (EAC, ECOWAS/WAEMU, SADC, CEMAC, North Africa) draws its regulatory, tax and language parameters from the [Africa Regulatory Dossiers](africa-regulatory/README.md); the playbook cites a dossier every time it names a country-specific step.
